Studies — research projects by Jofre Oliveras

Interspecies Archaeology (2025)

An archive for non-human agency: relics grown, eroded and indexed as if they were evidence of species that never signed their work.

Biomimetic Solar (2025)

Solar panels made from organic and regenerative materials through biomimetic design and DIY production methods — reimagining energy technology as ecological and cultural artefact.

Dialogue Between Civilizations (2025)

An atomic pyramid built from gold atoms, translating the geometric proportions of the pyramids of Giza into nanoscale structures — ancient architecture meets contemporary nanotechnology.

Fossil Synths (2026)

Functional PCB instruments in fossil form: manufacturable circuit boards whose copper is drawn as fossil anatomy, so electrical function and fossil form become one drawing.
